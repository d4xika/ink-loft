# Ink Loft 📚✨

Ink Loft is your personal sanctuary for everything books! It's a cozy digital corner where you can track your reading journey, curate your ever-growing library, and immortalize the quotes that touch your soul. 📖💖

The project is split into a Ruby on Rails API backend 🚂 and a responsive Vue.js frontend ⚡.

## Features 🪄

- **Book Tracking** 📚: Organize your library, keep track of your current reads, and celebrate every finished book in your reading history.
- **Quote Management** 🖋️: Save those sparkling gems of wisdom from your favorite books. Features a "Daily Quote" to inspire your inner bibliophile! ✨
- **Friends & Sharing** 🤝: Connect with friends, share quote libraries, and save books from their reading corners.
- **Activity Feed** 🔔: Follow friends' reading activity and see badges for unread updates and friend requests.
- **Themes** 🌓: Choose between light and dark mode for a comfortable reading experience.
- **User Authentication** 🔐: Your personal library is safe and sound with our secure login system.
- **Internationalization** 🌎: Read and track in multiple languages, because stories have no borders!

## The Ink Stack 🛠️

### Backend 🚂
- **Framework**: Ruby on Rails 8.1
- **Database**: PostgreSQL
- **Authentication**: BCrypt
- **Background Jobs**: Solid Queue
- **Caching**: Solid Cache
- **Deployment**: Docker / Kamal

### Frontend ⚡
- **Framework**: Vue.js 3 (Composition API)
- **UI Component Library**: PrimeVue
- **State Management**: Vue's built-in reactivity
- **Routing**: Vue Router
- **Validation**: Zod
- **Build Tool**: Vite
